## 📊 Featured Data & Product Projects

### 🛠️ 1. Bank-Wide KYC Data Cleansing Engine (MAB Bank)
*   **The Challenge:** Over 200,000 unstructured, unformatted unique customer ID records out of alignment with Central Bank of Myanmar (CBM) regulatory compliance.
*   **The Solution:** Rather than manual review, I built a pattern-recognition framework that clustered the messy data into 5,000 distinct systemic branch typos. I implemented statistical sampling (validating clusters with 10 random checks across 12 regions) to automate programmatic corrections (e.g., mapping `KaMaYa` to `KaMaRa`).
*   **Impact:** Cleaned **200,000+ IDs**, reduced data complexity by **97.5%**, and insulated the bank against strict regulatory penalties.

### 📈 2. Executive Portfolio & Credit Risk Dashboards (MAB Bank)
